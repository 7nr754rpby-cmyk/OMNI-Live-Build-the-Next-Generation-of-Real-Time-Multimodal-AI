# OMNI Live: Build the Next Generation of Real-Time Multimodal AI

## Brief Introduction

AI is moving beyond traditional text-based chat toward **real-time, multimodal interaction**.

**OMNI Live** challenges hackers to imagine what becomes possible when AI can **see, hear, speak, and interact with users in real time**.

Build a functional AI application for a real-world **edge-device scenario**—such as a phone, wearable, smart home device, robot, vehicle, or other interactive endpoint—using an OMNI multimodal model.

Your application may call the OMNI model through cloud APIs; **the model does not need to run locally on the device**. What matters is creating a compelling, responsive, and complete multimodal experience.

Teams are encouraged to explore capabilities such as:

● Vision and video understanding

● Speech and audio understanding

● Natural voice interaction

● Streaming and low-latency responses

● Multimodal contextual understanding

● Adaptive voice, tone, or expression

● Continuous or interruptible human–AI interaction

You do **not** need to use every OMNI capability. A focused, well-executed experience is better than an overly complex system.

## Eligibility

To be eligible for the **OMNI Live Sponsor Prize**, your team must:

● Build a **functional and demo-ready application prototype** during Hack the North.

● Use an **OMNI multimodal model**, such as Qwen3.5-Omni where applicable.

● Target a clear **real-world or edge-device scenario**, rather than building a generic chatbot.

● Meaningfully incorporate all three core modality categories:

○ **Vision / video**

○ **Speech / audio**

○ **Language**

● Demonstrate at least one complete end-to-end user scenario.

● Show meaningful interaction between the application and the OMNI model.

● Submit a project repository with basic setup/run instructions and required dependencies.

Applications may run on **web, mobile, desktop, HarmonyOS/Android devices, IoT devices, wearables, robots, vehicles, or simulated edge-device environments**.

The OMNI model itself may run in the cloud and be accessed through APIs. **Local model deployment, training, and fine-tuning are not required.**

Your submission should demonstrate why multimodal AI provides a meaningful advantage for the chosen scenario. Simply adding vision, audio, or voice features for technical completeness is not sufficient.

Concept-only designs, slide decks, and static mockups are **not eligible**; the core experience must be functional and demonstrable.

## Judging Criteria

Projects will be evaluated based on the following criteria:

### 1. Scenario Value & Creativity — 30%

Does the project address a clear user or real-world problem?

We are looking for original applications where multimodal AI enables an experience that would be difficult to achieve with a traditional chatbot or a vision-only / audio-only solution.

Strong projects should demonstrate a compelling reason for AI to **see, hear, speak, and understand context together**.

---

### 2. Use of OMNI Capabilities — 25%

How effectively does the project use OMNI's multimodal capabilities?

We will consider the quality and relevance of features such as:

● Speech and audio understanding

● Image and video understanding

● Multimodal contextual reasoning

● Streaming responses

● Natural voice interaction

● Voice, style, or tone adaptation

● Continuous or interruptible interaction

The modalities should work together meaningfully rather than being implemented as separate features.

---

### 3. Demo Completeness — 20%

Is the application functional, stable, and demo-ready?

We value a **small but complete experience** over a large system with unfinished features.

The project should demonstrate at least one clear end-to-end workflow in a live demo.

---

### 4. Interaction Experience — 15%

Does the interaction feel natural, responsive, and intuitive?

We will consider:

● Response latency

● Interaction flow

● Quality and clarity of feedback

● Multi-turn or continuous interaction

● How naturally voice, vision, and language work together

---

### 5. Technical Implementation — 10%

Is the technical architecture reasonable and well executed?

We will consider:

● Edge-to-cloud architecture

● Integration with the OMNI model

● System stability

● Data processing approach

● Technical clarity

Additional consideration may be given to thoughtful implementation of:

● **Multi-device collaboration**

● **Edge inference or local optimization**

● **Latency reduction and caching**

● **Privacy protection**

● **Safety-aware design**

---

## What We’re Looking For

A winning project does **not** need to use every OMNI capability.

We are looking for projects with:

● A specific and meaningful real-world scenario

● A clear user problem

● A compelling reason to use multimodal AI

● Meaningful integration of vision/video, speech/audio, and language

● A complete end-to-end interaction flow

● Natural and responsive real-time interaction

● A functional and stable live demo

● A clear edge-device experience

● Thoughtful technical implementation

● Potential for future development and real-world impact

Teams are encouraged to build a **small but complete experience** rather than an overly ambitious system.

Most importantly, we want to see a project that answers one question:

**When AI can see, hear, speak, and interact with users in real time, what can you build that was not possible with traditional applications?**
